# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-17

First public release.

- Ctrl-click (Cmd or Shift also work) a column header to add it as an extra sort level; click again to reverse, a third time to remove.
- Header markers show each level's position and direction.
- Works on Soldiers > Manage, Recruit and Memorial, and in the dropship soldier picker.
- Soldier Total Stats' TOT column can be used as a level when that mod is installed.
